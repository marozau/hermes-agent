Create a sprint plan from the epics and stories:

1. **Sprint Goal** — What the sprint aims to deliver
2. **Story Selection** — Stories from the backlog for this sprint
3. **Capacity Planning** — Estimated velocity vs story point totals
4. **Dependencies** — Story ordering, prerequisites
5. **Risk Assessment** — What could delay the sprint, mitigation strategies
6. **Definition of Done** — Quality gates for each story

Format each story as:
- Story ID and title
- Priority (P0-P3)
- Effort estimate
- Assigned persona/role
- Acceptance criteria

Write to `planning-artifacts/sprint-planning-{project_name}-{date}.md`.

Also update `planning-artifacts/sprint-status.yaml` with the planned stories.
